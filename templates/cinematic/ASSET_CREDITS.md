Trees, rocks, grass, flowers, mushrooms, logs, the backpack, the lantern, light shafts and dust are generated in code (original, no external asset). So are the P4 workshop (floor, walls, clocks, shelves, counter, lamp), the studio floor, the pocket watch, the falling clock and all textures of those. Sound effects and music of the P4 story are synthesised by `templates/audio/story_sound.py`. The placeholder narration is spoken by the free Windows voice on the user's own PC (the speech is the user's output; no recording is included in the repository).
See `asset-manifest.json` for exact files, checksums and notes.

History: the P1 version of this scene used the KayKit Adventurers "Ranger" (Kay Lousberg, CC0). It was replaced in P1.1 because its chibi proportions could not be made adult, and it is no longer used.
